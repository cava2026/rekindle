# Burnout Buddy

**Less on your mind. More space for you.**

Burnout Buddy is a personal operating system for sustainable wellbeing. Our vision is to help people turn overwhelm into clarity, manageable action, and alignment with what matters to them, before exhaustion becomes burnout.

## Product vision

Burnout Buddy combines immediate support through **Buddy Reset** with a proactive approach to wellbeing through **Buddy Align**.

### Buddy Reset

A memorable method for working through overwhelm, one step at a time:

| Step | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **B — Brain Dump** | Unload thoughts, worries, and competing responsibilities. |
| **U — Understand and Unpack** | Explore what is creating overwhelm. |
| **D — Decide** | Choose a realistic priority based on current capacity. |
| **D — Do** | Take one small, meaningful action. |
| **Y — Your Why** | Connect that action to personal values and goals. |

### Buddy Align

Buddy Align is intended to connect everyday reflections, goals, calendar patterns, and health signals with practical guidance for a healthier, more sustainable life.

## Who it is for

Our initial audience is independent women navigating major life changes while carrying multiple responsibilities across work, relationships, health, and personal goals.

They need a compassionate companion that understands **capacity, not just commitment**, without adding another demanding productivity routine.

## Intended user experience

Burnout Buddy aims to offer a clean, comforting, affordable, and simple experience. Users can:

- Unload mental clutter and organize competing priorities.
- Reflect on stress patterns across different areas of life.
- Identify small next steps aligned with personal values and goals.
- Complete lightweight check-ins and take one meaningful action at a time.

The goal is sustainable progress, rather than maximum productivity.

Optional calendar, wearable, and manual check-in integrations are part of the product vision. These would help users explore patterns in sleep, workload, recovery, activity, and wellbeing without receiving medical diagnoses.

## Our approach

We aim to bring commitments, emotions, behavior, environment, and personal data into one accessible experience. Our intended distinction is the combination of mental health-informed reflection, personalized planning, and privacy-conscious technology.

This is our product positioning, not a claim that no comparable products exist.

## Team

Burnout Buddy grew from a four-person founding team working without a dedicated technical builder and with little prior hands-on AI experience. The team brings perspectives from medicine, engineering, UX design, and diplomacy, with a shared focus on understanding users and learning through experimentation.

## Project status

This repository supports the Burnout Buddy hackathon project. This README describes the product vision and intended experience; it does not imply that every feature is already implemented.

Confirmed working features, setup instructions, technical dependencies, and demo links will be documented as the implementation is verified.

## Roadmap

Planned future capabilities include:

- Privacy-preserving personalization.
- Richer wearable integrations.
- Identification of patterns related to resilience and recovery.
- User-controlled data sharing.
- Wellness summaries that users can choose to share with care providers.

## Responsible use and privacy

Burnout Buddy is intended to provide educational support, reflection, and everyday wellbeing guidance. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions, replace professional care, or guarantee burnout prevention.

Privacy-preserving personalization and user control over data are design goals. Specific protections and integration permissions must be documented and verified as these features are implemented.

Do not commit API keys, credentials, or personal health data to this public repository.
